Note: Door filename is constructed by concatinating RoomId and ExitToRoomId.
      The lesser of the two is first, like SmallHouse233.SouthEnd26.txt because
      'SmallHouse233' is less than 'SouthEnd26'.

Door States
  Open   NoLock
  Open   Unlocked
  Closed NoLock
  Closed Unlocked
  Closed Locked

Door State Transistions
  OpenNoLock     -> Close  -> ClosedNoLock
  OpenUnlocked   -> Close  -> CloseUnlocked
  ClosedNoLock   -> Open   -> OpenNoLock
  ClosedUnlocked -> Open   -> OpenUnlocked
  ClosedUnlocked -> Lock   -> ClosedLocked
  ClosedLocked   -> Unlock -> ClosedUnlocked
